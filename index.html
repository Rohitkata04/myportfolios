Founder & Product Lead
HaamIn

Making ownership of every product digital, verifiable and connected across its lifecycle.

Visit haamin.com Pitch deck

HaamIn gives every product one verifiable ownership record — from the retailer's digital bill at the point of sale through its warranty, service history and resale — replacing the scattered paper bill, WhatsApp warranty and emailed receipt. Nothing gets lost, warranties stop lapsing unused, and the full ownership lifecycle stays connected in one place.

One verifiable record for every product a customer owns
Reminders before warranties expire, renew in one tap
Book verified technicians; every repair recorded
Connects retailers, customers, technicians and warranty providers
Traction: validated with 52 customers & 29 retailers in Hyderabad (~90% interest); first MVP retailer committed. Status: research & revenue model done, prototype in build, IP filed (Class 42).
